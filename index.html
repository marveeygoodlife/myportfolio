Who I am


      

        

        

          

            I’ve always been the kind of person who learns by doing. As a kid, I used to make my own little light bulbs
            — wiring LEDs to batteries just to see them glow. I’d fix wall sockets, change lamp holders. I didn’t think
            of it as “learning” back then; I was just curious to understand how things worked and how to make them better.
          


          

            That same curiosity drives me today as a self-taught web developer. I’m drawn to building things that work —
            clean, responsive websites that connect logic with design. Every new project teaches me something: a better way
            to write code, a smarter layout, a simpler solution.
          


          

            For me, learning has never been about perfection — it’s about consistent progress. I’m always exploring new ideas,
            breaking them down, and rebuilding them to understand them more deeply. Web development is my craft, but learning
            is my lifelong habit.
